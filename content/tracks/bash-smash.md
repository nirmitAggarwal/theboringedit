Welcome to the track. Every part here is dedicated to the strange little world where computers stop being pretty and start being honest — the terminal. Bash has been quietly holding together servers, deployments, automation, and questionable late-night engineering decisions for decades, while GUIs take all the credit. Here, you'll learn to talk to your machine directly: navigating files, chaining commands, moving data around, writing scripts, and eventually making the computer do the boring stuff for you.

The rule of the track: every part must be a *usable* deep dive. No memorising a dictionary of flags you'll immediately forget — you'll learn by running commands, breaking things, fixing them, and building small projects along the way.
